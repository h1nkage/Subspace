If you somehow missed dragging your disc image file directly into Subspace, or you otherwise messed up your games directory or got lost
and ended up here, don't worry. All you have to do still is move your "Super Smash Bros. Brawl (USA)" file to this folder.

Subspace supports either Rev 1 or Rev 2, and .iso/.rvz/.wbfs/.wia/.gcz extensions.
